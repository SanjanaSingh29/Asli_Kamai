# Asli_Kamai

# 🛵 Asli Kamai (असली कमाई)

**What delivery riders and drivers actually take home.**

Asli Kamai is a Streamlit app that shows gig workers their *real* hourly
earnings after the costs of running their own vehicle: fuel, bike EMI,
maintenance and depreciation.

## The problem

Gig platforms show gross payouts, like "₹1,500 earned today." What they
don't show is how much of that goes into petrol, the bike loan, servicing
and wear and tear. Riders often can't tell which platform, shift or time
slot actually pays best once their own costs are counted.

## The solution

Upload your earnings (or try the sample data) and enter your vehicle costs.
Asli Kamai calculates:

- **Net earnings per hour**, not just gross
- **A cost breakdown** from gross payout to take-home, as a waterfall chart
- **Platform comparison** across Blinkit, Zepto, Rapido and others
- **Best days and time slots**, shown as a heatmap
- **What-if scenarios**: "What if I work 2 hours less?" or "What if petrol goes up ₹10?"

## How it's different

Existing gig-earnings trackers (Gridwise, Solo and similar) are built
around US tax and mileage rules. Asli Kamai is built for Indian riders:
costs in rupees, bike EMI and per-km maintenance, and comparison across
Indian delivery and ride platforms.

## Who it's for

Delivery partners and ride-hailing drivers who own their vehicle and want
to know whether their hours are actually worth it.

## Note on the sample data

`data/sample_earnings.csv` is synthetic, created to demo the app. It does
not represent real rider earnings.
